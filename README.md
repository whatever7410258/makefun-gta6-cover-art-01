Cover Snap — a GTA 6-style cover art maker. Turn your own photos and a caption into a multi-panel Vice City sunset poster with bold caption lettering, and export it as a PNG to share. Static files only, works fully offline in desktop Chrome; photos never leave your machine.

## What it does

- **Any photo, any panel:** load up to three photos from your machine (or try the two built-in samples), then choose which photo fills each panel — one photo across all panels, or a different one per panel.
- **Crop and position:** zoom and slide each photo inside its panel with sliders, or just drag the photo on the preview.
- **Three poster layouts:** Sunset Grid (hero + two panels), Neon Trio (three tall panels), Hero + Strip (big hero over three small panels).
- **Vice City colour grades:** Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Palm Dusk and Midnight Noir — each with its own sunset wash, sun disc and caption glow.
- **Bold lettering:** the caption is set in Anton (SIL Open Font License, shipped under `app/fonts/`), in solid, neon-glow or sunset-gradient styles, with an editable sub-caption.
- **Post sizes:** export a PNG at 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 or 1920 x 1080 — three steps from photo to file.

Everything is original: no Rockstar logo, wordmark, artwork, character or font. The only font is Anton, and the sample photos are the kit's own AI-made images.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
